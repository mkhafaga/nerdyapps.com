Sound Match

A memory game for kids where every card plays a sound.

On Google Play · App Store coming soon

Get it onGoogle Play

How it plays

Tap a card to flip it and hear its sound, then find its pair. Children match animals, instruments and vehicles by picture and by sound. Boards grow from 4 to 16 cards as they get better.

  • No reading neededPictures and sounds guide the way.
  • No timer, no losingPlay at your own pace.
  • Works offlineEverything is on the device.
  • No ads, no trackingNo accounts and no data collected.

For grown-ups

Sound, vibration and board size are in a settings area behind a simple grown-ups check, so little hands stay in the game. Read the privacy policy.
